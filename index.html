GAME PREVIEW

The platform will feature exactly five distinct games. Until game concepts and rules are finalized by the Sphinx committee, the temporary designations Game 01 through Game 05 are used.

COMPETITION STRUCTURE

GAME LEADERBOARDS

Each game will have its own dedicated leaderboard. Multiple levels or stages may exist within a game, but there will be no separate leaderboard for each level.

GENERAL PARTICIPATION FLOW

HOW TO PLAY

Review the confirmed four-step flow for participating in Blackout: Restore the Grid.

01

Register

Register with your Full Name, College ID, and College Name to begin.

02

Choose a Game

Select one of the five available games (Game 01 through Game 05) to initiate.

03

Play the Game

Complete the challenges within the selected game. Games may include multiple stages.

04

View Leaderboard

Check your score on that game's dedicated leaderboard upon completion.

NOTICE: Registration and gameplay are not active in this phase. Game-specific rules, scoring systems, and stage mechanics will be provided when the games are finalized.
